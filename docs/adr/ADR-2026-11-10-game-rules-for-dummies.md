# The rules of Bluff, in plain English

- **Status:** Approved
- **Technical version:** [ADR-2026-11-10-game-rules.md](ADR-2026-11-10-game-rules.md)

## What is the problem?

We need rules that are fun, quick to play, and fair in a way anyone can check, while keeping each player's dice secret until the right moment.

## What are we doing about it?

Two to four players sit at a table, each with three dice (or five for a longer game). Everyone's dice are rolled by the game itself, and only you can see yours. Ones are wild.

Taking turns, each player makes a guess about all the dice on the table, like "four 5s", and every guess must be bigger than the last. If you think the last guess is a lie, you call the bluff. Everyone's dice are then shown. If the guess was true, you lose a die. If it was a lie, the person who made it loses one. Lose all your dice and you are out. The last player standing wins all the chips on the table.

Each turn has 90 seconds, so nobody can freeze a game by walking away.

## What changes for people?

Games take a few minutes. Nobody, including us, can see or change your dice, and when a bluff is called, everyone sees the proof of every die.

## What could go wrong?

Every move takes a few seconds to be confirmed, so a long game could drag. Small tables and three dice each keep games short.
