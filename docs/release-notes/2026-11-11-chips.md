# Chips: the faucet, paid tables, the paid-in-full check and payouts

Players can claim 100 free practice chips a day, pay and join a table in one move, and have their payment checked privately before the first roll. Leaving the waiting room refunds the buy-in, and the pot goes to the winner, or is shared between tied winners, when the game ends.

## Changes

- `BluffCashier` (new UUPS proxy) holds every chip, following [ADR-2026-11-11-bluff-cashier](../adr/ADR-2026-11-11-bluff-cashier.md).
  - It is the ERC-7984 receiver: a pay-and-join call to Zama's chip token reaches the cashier, which records `paid`, `paidInFull` and the pot.
  - It refunds and pays out when `Bluff` tells it to.
  - A payment that is not exactly the buy-in is handed back by the token automatically.
- `Bluff` holds no chips.
  - `seatPaidPlayer` and `startIfFull` accept calls only from the cashier, linked once by `setCashier`.
  - A full paid table moves to `Verifying`, the paid-in-full flags are made public, and `confirmSeats` checks the proof. It removes unpaid seats and reopens the table, or rolls round 1.
  - `cancelVerification` refunds everyone after 10 minutes.
  - Leaving the waiting room or the check refunds; cancelling a waiting table refunds everyone; every way a game ends pays out, with the remainder of an uneven split going to the first player to have joined.
- `ChipFaucet.claim(nickname)` mints mock USDT and wraps it to the player, 100 chips every 24 hours, using the token's `rate()`. `setNickname` changes the nickname.
- The deploy task deploys three proxies and links the cashier; the upgrade task accepts `BluffCashier`.
- `Bluff` is compiled with 50 optimizer runs to keep 2 KB under the contract size limit.
- Tests: `faucet.test.ts`, `callback.test.ts`, `seat-verification.test.ts`, `payouts.test.ts`, and cashier cases in the admin, upgrade and task tests.

## Removed

- `Bluff.chips()` and the chip token argument of `Bluff.initialize`, replaced by `cashier()` and `setCashier`.
- The encrypted payment fields on `Bluff`'s seats and tables, now in the cashier.
