# The lobby: find a seat, invite friends, and a sign-in flow that never shows the wrong page

Players can now sit down at a table. **Play now** finds a seat in one tap, and players can also create a free or paid table, browse the open ones, or follow an invite link. New players always go sign-in → welcome → lobby, and no page ever flashes where it shouldn't. Form problems arrive as short toasts that say what to do, and the lobby sits over a shadowy Nigerian street scene.

## Changes

- **Lobby (`features/lobby`):**
  - **Play now** joins the fullest open table with the chosen chips to play, or opens a public table for two.
  - **Chips to play:** Free, 10, 25 or 50. A paid choice the player can't afford holds Play now with "You need 25 chips for this table".
  - **Create a table:** a dialog with 2, 3 or 4 players (showing the dice each player gets), the chips to play, and "Only people with the link".
  - **Open tables:** a yellow button with a live count badge opens a wide modal of table cards. When nothing is open, it shows an illustrated empty table, "Every seat is empty right now", Play now and Create a table.
  - **Invites:** links look like `/play?join=12`, so they work before the waiting room exists and survive sign-in. **Have a link? Paste it here** accepts a link or a table number.
  - **Your table:** the seats and their state, **Copy invite link** while seats are free, and **Leave the table**, which asks first once a game has started.
  - **Paid tables:** joining pays the exact sealed buy-in through `confidentialTransferAndCall` to the cashier.
- **Sign-in and routing:**
  - Sign-in waits until the player's wallet exists, then sends new players to `/welcome` and returning players to where they were going.
  - Wallet creation runs through one shared step, so it happens exactly once per player. A stuck account without a wallet gets one on the next game page.
  - Game pages draw nothing until the player is known and allowed there; until then they show "Getting your seat ready…".
  - The server sends signed-in visitors away from `/sign-in` and `/refresh` before anything is drawn. Google sign-in returning to the sign-in page is never interrupted. A stale session is closed before sending the player to sign in, with **Try again** if it won't close.
  - The welcome screen says **Save** instead of "Save and get 100 chips" when today's chips were already claimed, and saves the name and face without trying to claim.
- **Header:**
  - The player's face and name are a raised button that opens a player modal with Sign out.
  - The chips are a button that opens the chips card as a dialog, with a pulsing dot when free chips or an unfinished cash out are waiting.
  - The header spans the full width with side padding.
- **Feedback and look:**
  - Every button has an icon; a test fails if any game button lacks one.
  - Form problems are short error toasts that say what to do. Every toast leaves by itself (success 3 s, info 4 s, error 6 s) and pauses while hovered.
  - Modals are wider and blur the page behind them.
  - The lobby sits over a cartoon street (houses, palms, old men playing ayo, a trader, a child) drawn as one soft shadow, with lit windows at night. It sinks below the bottom edge, and the card stays centred.
  - The street filter and pattern drawings use fixed ids, so the shadow survives moving between pages without a reload.
- **Errors:**
  - Moves through the chip token decode every game error, so a refused paid join says why.
  - New sentences for `InvalidUnwrapRequest`, and from PR 36 for `TableFull` and `NotEveryoneReady`.
- **Sign-in screen:** Google and email are the two main buttons; "I already have a wallet" is a quiet link.
- **Docs:** the engineering standards' toast rules (6 s errors, hover pause, input problems as toasts) and the plan's test list.

## Removed

- `LobbyWelcome` and its "Your tables will appear here soon" line, replaced by `PlayerGreeting` inside the lobby card.
- The welcome screen's own redirect and its `next` prop; the route gate alone sends players on.
- The market backdrop and its colour tokens, replaced by the street scene.
- Privy's automatic wallet creation on login (`createOnLogin: "off"`), replaced by the explicit step.

## Verification

- **Tests:** 328 web tests and 199 contract tests passing, with red then green for every fix:
  - wallet creation before leaving sign-in, exactly one wallet, routing new and returning players;
  - the gate never drawing the wrong page, the stale-session loop;
  - signed-in visitors skipping sign-in;
  - the street shadow after moving between pages;
  - toasts leaving and pausing;
  - the welcome **Save** path;
  - a full waiting table saying everyone is here;
  - refusals decoded through the chip token.
- **Used by the maintainer on `localhost:3002`:**
  - Google, email and own-wallet sign-in, and a brand-new email (one wallet, welcome, claim from the header, then **Save**);
  - free and paid create, join by invite link, and leave across two accounts;
  - reloads while seated, typing `/welcome` and `/sign-in` while signed in, and the shadowy street after signing in.
- **Not covered yet:** notices to the other players when someone joins, leaves, becomes host or changes their ready mark. These come with the waiting room in the next PR.
