# The landing page, and sharing

PR 6-2 of the [learn and watch plan](../plans/2026-11-23-learn-and-watch-plan.md). **No contract change, no new package.**

## Changes

- **The landing page (`/`)** replaces the bare title and two lines, which had no way into the game:
  - the tumbling die beside the name and the tagline, and a one-line explainer that mentions playing friends, anyone online or the computer;
  - **one way in:** a large raised **Sit down and play** link to `/play`, which sends anyone not signed in to sign-in first;
  - **three tilting cards** for the moves of the game, each with a small picture: dice for **Roll in secret**, a bid marker for **Bid boldly**, a megaphone for **Call the bluff**;
  - a fairness line with links to **How to play** and **Why it's fair**;
  - the street backdrop and screen controls, like the rest of the game. The page scrolls inside itself on small screens.
- **A live game** beside the title plays a looping demo round in about 12 seconds, on the **real 3D board** with the game's own seats:
  - four players (Bluff's own faces) sit round the board, and every cup shakes as the dice are rolled. **Your** cup throws your dice onto the board with the game's own physics (`useGameBoard`, with a new `quiet` option so the loop makes no sound), and your seat shows your dice, as in a real game;
  - bids go round as each seat's bid pill, with the turn ring moving and the bid marker above climbing (2, 3, then 4 × five), and **You** shouts "Bluff!";
  - the cups lift, your thrown dice clear away, everyone's dice are laid out with the fives and wild ones glowing, and only three count, so Femi loses a die, using the game's own lost-die animation;
  - then a new round starts: your cup throws fresh dice, and Femi has both dice back.

  It's a real round under the rules, with one spoken description for screen readers, and it holds still on the reveal under calm motion. It follows the player's board setting (3D, or the flat board), and the 3D code loads lazily after the page. The demo lives in the `table` feature (`DemoGame`), and the route hands it to the landing page's `Hero` as a slot, so no feature imports another.

- **How to play and Why it's fair are raised tiles everywhere** (`LearnLinks`): yellow for the rules, blue for fairness, each with an icon. They're under the cards on the landing page, and in the lobby, and the learn pages' own buttons use the same colours. They replace the small text links.
- **Fix on the real 3D board: left and right seats' revealed dice now sit inside the board.** Each revealed row was moved "forward" from its seat by turning an offset with the seat's facing, which works for the top and bottom seats but sends the left and right seats' rows outward, onto the board's edge. It affected every real 3- and 4-player game, not just the demo. The row is now pulled straight from the cup towards the middle (`lib/revealed-row.ts`), whatever the seat's facing. The flat board was never affected.
- **The landing page fits on one screen with no scrolling,** at the maintainer's request:
  - the three move cards sit as a compact row under **Sit down and play**;
  - the live game is sized by the screen's height;
  - the fairness line and **How to play** and **Why it's fair** share one row at the bottom.

  On phones, the explainer line and the cards are left out, since the live game shows the same thing.

- **Sharing:**
  - `app/opengraph-image.tsx` makes a 1200 × 630 share image with `next/og` (part of Next): the name, the tagline and four dice in the seat colours, in the night palette;
  - links shared on X now show the large image card (`summary_large_image`);
  - the sitemap lists `/`, `/how-to-play` and `/fairness`. Game pages stay out of it and stay `noindex`.
- **The share image's font:** Next's built-in font is used, because the brand font loads from Google at runtime and adding its file to the repository was not part of this change.

## Removed

- The old hero layout and its tests, replaced by the new hero and its tests.

## Verification

- **Vitest:**
  - the hero names the game, has one link into it, shows the three moves in order, and links to the rules and fairness pages;
  - the sitemap lists exactly the three public pages.
  - the demo round plays its steps in order and loops, and holds still on the reveal under calm motion;
  - a quiet board shakes and throws without a sound; each loop counts as a new round;
  - the demo game shakes every cup and throws your dice on the real board to deal, shows your dice at your seat, gives the loser their dice back next round, raises the bid each turn, has you call the bluff, lifts the cups with exactly three dice counting, then shows who lost;
  - the hero shows the table it is given;
  - every seat's revealed row lies between its cup and the middle (red first: the left and right seats failed with the old maths);
  - `LearnLinks` gives two raised tile links to the right pages.
- **Smoke test:** the production build serves `/opengraph-image` as a PNG, and the landing page carries `og:image` and the large X card tag.
- **Live, on a desktop screen, day and night:** the page fits the screen with nothing to scroll (content height equals screen height); Kola's and Femi's revealed dice sit inside the board; the page, the live game on the 3D board through whole rounds, and the share image look right, with no console errors.
- **Not verified:** a phone-sized screen. The browser window couldn't be resized, and the security headers rightly refuse framing. The layout stacks the cards and scrolls inside itself on small screens.
