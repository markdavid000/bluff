# Welcome: pick your name and face, and get your first chips

A new player is welcomed before anything else. They pick a name (one is suggested, and **Suggest another** offers more) and one of 12 African faces. One move saves both and gives them their first 100 practice chips. Their face and name then show in the header.

## Changes

- **`ChipFaucet` 1.1.0**, appending to `bluff.storage.ChipFaucet`:
  - `claimWithAvatar(nickname, avatar)`, which saves the name and face and gives the daily chips in one move;
  - `setAvatar(1..12)`;
  - `setAvatarImage(cid)`, ready for uploads later;
  - `avatarOf(player)`;
  - the `AvatarSet` event, and the `InvalidAvatar` and `InvalidAvatarImage` errors;
  - `AvatarRules`, holding the 12 faces and the safe characters for a picture address.
- **`useSendMove`:**
  1. simulates every move first, so a bad move is caught for free, with its reason;
  2. sends it sponsored for Google and email players, through Privy, or through the player's own wallet;
  3. waits for confirmation.
- **`useAction`**, the shared status model: preparing, confirming, sending, waiting, then done or failed.
  - One toast per action, shown after 300 ms for at least 500 ms, with a busy note after 15 s.
  - A plain-language failure with **Try again**; a wallet cancel is a short note.
- **`lib/errors.ts`** turns every custom error into a sentence from `lib/copy.ts`.
- **`lib/notify.ts`** is now a small message bus that any layer may use, and `GameToaster` draws it as wooden tiles.
- `/welcome`:
  - a name box, with validation matching `NicknameRules` and suggestions such as "Brave Lion";
  - a grid of 12 faces;
  - one **Save and get 100 chips** button, which says "Confirm in your wallet" only for own-wallet players.
- **First-visit gate:** a signed-in player without a name is taken to `/welcome`, then on to where they were going.
- **Header:** the player's face beside their name. Players who never chose a face get one picked from their address.
- **12 faces** in `public/avatars`, from Open Peeps (CC0) through DiceBear, credited in `public/CREDITS.md`.
- Icon names live in `config/icons.ts`, so every layer can name an icon.
- **Night mode:** plain tiles are warm mahogany with a wooden edge, so they stand apart from the indigo background. Every tile and card has a thin top highlight, so the raised look shows in the dark.
- **Suggest another** shows "Thinking…" for a moment, then slides the new name in.
- **A match-up sits above the welcome card, over a soft glow:**
  - the player's face on the left, which pops in as they pick one;
  - the tumbling 3D die in the middle;
  - a fixed opponent's face on the right.
- **The tumbling die** now has tight edges: square-ish corners, no outer shadow, and faces that meet, so the cube shows no gaps.
- **Fixed pages.** The sign-in and game pages are exactly the height of the screen and never scroll. The header stays at the top, and each card is centred in the space below. On very small phones, the card itself can scroll as a last resort. The sign-in page on phones drops the three extra cards so it fits.
- **Sign-in glow:** now uses `--glow-top` and `--glow-bottom`, so the night glow is a soft, cooler lantern light instead of the heavy daytime yellow and brown.
- **Live on Sepolia.** `ChipFaucet` was upgraded in place to 1.1.0, at implementation `0xc0276c42C793F86c7ad4e6a5aD91E5C9F8eF49ac`, verified on Etherscan and linked to the proxy.
- **Upgrade task fix.** The first run of `upgrade:sepolia` upgraded correctly but read the old implementation and version back from a node that was behind, and recorded them. `tasks/chain.ts` fixes this for both tasks:
  - the upgrade task now gets the target implementation from `prepareUpgrade`;
  - it waits until the proxy points there;
  - it reads `version()` at that block;
  - the deploy task reads at the deployment's confirmed block.

## Removed

- `lucide-react`. The music menu's dot and tick are now 3D icons (`music`, and a new `tick` from 3dicons, CC0). The shadcn dropdown items take an `indicator` instead of drawing Lucide icons themselves.
- The old `notify` module that rendered toasts itself, replaced by the bus and `GameToaster`.
