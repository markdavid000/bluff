# Change your name and face anytime

Pressing your name in the header now opens your profile, where you can change your name or your face, open the game tips, or sign out. PR 10-1 of the [profile and history plan](../plans/2026-11-17-profile-and-history-plan.md).

## Changes

- **Your profile:** the header dialog shows your face and name, with **Change name**, **Change face**, **Game tips** and **Sign out**. On phones, it also has the day/night, sound and music controls, as before.
- **Change name:**
  - It uses the same field and rules as the welcome screen.
  - A taken name gets a toast as you type, and Save stays off.
  - It goes back to the profile once the name is saved.
  - An unchanged name goes straight back.
- **Change face:** the same 12 faces as the welcome screen. Pressing one saves it and goes back. The faces are locked while it saves.
- **Saving:** the dialog stays open while a change saves, with a "still going" toast, and reopens on the profile.
- **Shared pieces:** the name field (`NicknameInput`) and the face grid (`FacePicker`) moved out of the welcome screen into shared pieces, which both screens use. The welcome screen is unchanged for players.

## Removed

- The welcome screen's own copies of the name field and the face grid, replaced by the shared pieces.
