# Share previews, canonical addresses, icons and table titles

Found in the PR 7 audit. **No contract change, no new package.**

## Changes

- **Share previews for every public page.** A page's share settings replace the layout's wholesale. How to play and Why it's fair set none, so they inherited the home page's title, description and address "/", and a link to `/fairness` previewed as the home page. A `pageMetadata()` helper now gives the home page, How to play and Why it's fair each their own:
  - title, description and **canonical address**;
  - Open Graph title, description, address and the share image;
  - X's large image card.

  The root layout keeps only the defaults (title template, description, robots) and no longer sets an address.

- **The share image is named explicitly.** The generated image only applied automatically to the home page, so the other public pages had no preview image until the helper named it.
- **A favicon and a phone home-screen icon** (`app/icon.tsx`, `app/apple-icon.tsx`): a red die showing five, made with `next/og` like the share image. There was no site icon at all before.
- **Table pages are titled "Table 123456"** instead of "Table", so browser tabs and history tell games apart. They stay out of search engines.

## Verification

- **Vitest:**
  - the helper gives a page its own title, description, canonical address, share preview and image, and the plain name for the home page;
  - table titles name the table, with a plain fallback.
- **Smoke test (production build):** `/how-to-play` and `/fairness` carry their own `og:url`, canonical link, `og:image` and the large X card, and `/icon` and `/apple-icon` are PNGs.
- **Checked by hand:** the home page has exactly one `og:image`, and the icons look right.
