# Lifted cups slide back, so your revealed dice are never hidden

## Changes

- **On the 3D board, a lifted cup now slides back 0.9, straight away from the middle, as well as rising** (`lib/lifted-cup.ts`). The camera looks at the board from your seat, so your lifted cup used to hover in front of your own revealed dice and hide them. It now moves out of the way, in real games and in the landing demo.
- **Every seat's revealed dice now sit 0.85 in from its cup.** The special case that kept your own pair further in (1.3), so your cup wouldn't hide it, is removed. Neighbouring pairs are now at least 2.3 apart, up from 2.0.

## Removed

- `NEAR_ROW_TOWARD_CENTRE` and its special case for the near seat.

## Verification

- **Vitest:**
  - every seat's lifted cup moves straight back from the middle by 0.9;
  - your revealed dice sit at least 1.6 in front of your lifted cup;
  - neighbouring revealed pairs are at least 2.3 apart.
- **Live:** in the landing demo's reveal, your own pair shows fully above your cup, and all four lifted cups sit back near the board's edges.
