# Watch a game

PR 6-3 of the [learn and watch plan](../plans/2026-11-23-learn-and-watch-plan.md), the last part of PR 6. Anyone signed in can follow a public game being played. **No contract change, no new package.**

## Changes

- **Lobby:** a **Watch a game** button beside Open tables, with a count of games being played. It opens a list of game cards, each with:
  - the seats, the round, the dice on the table and the chips;
  - a "With the computer" badge when the computer is playing;
  - a **Watch** link to the table.

  The list comes from `useGamesInPlay`, which reads `GameStarted` from the last 150 blocks (about 30 minutes, more than the 15-minute game clock), then each table. It keeps public games still playing or revealing, newest first, and leaves out the player's own (`liveGames`). It refreshes every 10 s, with loading, empty and error states.

- **The table page:** anyone not seated at a table that's playing, revealing or finished now gets the game screen as a **watcher**, instead of the join screen:
  - the board with every cup closed, the bids, the turn ring, dice counts, the round history and the reveals, exactly as players see them;
  - a **Watching** pill, and **Back to the lobby** in place of **Leave**;
  - no bid controls, no dice, and no keeper moves. The keeper gives nobody unseated a duty, as the web app ADR requires;
  - at the end, the winner and **Back to the lobby** only, without Play again or a seat at the next table.

  Waiting tables, and paid tables being checked, still show the join screen.

- **The turn line:** a watcher sees whose turn it is ("Ada is thinking…"), not "You're out of dice".
- **`TileLink`** takes an optional spoken label, so each card's **Watch** link names its table.
- **Docs:** the rules (`docs/how-to-play.md` and the How to play page) mention **Watch a game**.

## Removed

- The rule that a visitor to a game in progress sees the join screen saying the game has started. Watching replaces it, and its test is replaced by the watching tests.

## Verification

- **Vitest:**
  - `liveGames`: public in-play games only, no private, waiting, finished or cancelled tables, no own games, one card per table, and the computer badge;
  - `useGamesInPlay`: newest first, the block range, own games left out, and an error with retry;
  - the button and dialog: the count, the cards, the Watch link, and the empty, loading and error states;
  - the lobby button;
  - the table screen sends visitors to the game while it's playing, revealing or finished, and to the join screen while it's waiting or being checked;
  - the turn line for watchers;
  - the watcher's header: Watching, Back to the lobby, no Leave, no bid controls;
  - the watcher's results: no Play again or next-table offer.
- **Live:** the maintainer watched a game and asked for the shorter "Watching" pill.
